About Me

ABOUT THE PROFESSIONAL

I am a Registered Civil Engineer with professional experience in construction estimation, quantity surveying, structural engineering, and project site operations.

My experience includes preparing detailed material, labor, and equipment cost estimates; conducting quantity takeoffs for residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects; verifying construction quantities; and supporting project planning and execution.

I utilize industry-relevant software such as PlanSwift, Bluebeam Revu, Buildxact, Microsoft Excel, AutoCAD, STAAD.Pro, RCDC, and Primavera P6 to support accurate estimation, engineering documentation, structural analysis, and project monitoring.

With a strong foundation in civil engineering and practical construction experience, I aim to deliver reliable, organized, and cost-conscious engineering solutions.
